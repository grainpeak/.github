# Grainpeak LLC

Software development for robotics simulation and developer tools.

[Website](https://grainpeak.com) · [Company](https://grainpeak.com/company/) · [Email](mailto:contact@grainpeak.com)

Grainpeak LLC is a software development company registered in Wyoming, United States. We are developing robotics simulation software and tools for building software agents.

## Products and projects

### Robot simulation and development

We are developing a workflow that starts with virtual robot assembly and software development, then prepares configurations and exports for physical builds. The project remains in development; the complete manufacturing, assembly, and real-hardware workflow requires further validation.

[Project overview](https://grainpeak.com/project/)

### IsAnythingDown

[IsAnythingDown](https://isanythingdown.com/) is a free public early version operated by Grainpeak LLC. It brings together official status reports and recent history from artificial intelligence (AI) service providers. Reports reflect the scope of published sources and collection times.

## Company stage

We are in the pre-revenue stage, with no paying customers or commercial sales. Commercial scope, pricing, and a release date for the robotics project have not been finalized.
